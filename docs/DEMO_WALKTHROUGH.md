# Demo Walkthrough (6-8 minutes)

## 0) Opening statement (30 sec)

"RoadWatch AI is a civic intelligence platform that combines AI road damage detection, public spending transparency, and automated complaint workflows through a chatbot-first interface."

## 1) Dashboard + map story (1.5 min)

1. Open Home tab.
2. Show color-coded roads (red/yellow/green).
3. Highlight road health score and nearby complaints.
4. Show transparency card: budget allocated vs actual condition.

Judge angle:

- real-time visibility
- accountability signal
- practical city-level prioritization

## 2) AI detection flow (1.5 min)

1. Open Capture tab.
2. Tap `Run Demo: Pothole`.
3. Show bounding boxes + severity + score.
4. Tap `Generate Complaint from Detection`.

Judge angle:

- AI evidence
- rapid reporting
- less manual friction

## 3) Chatbot as primary interface (1 min)

Ask:

- "Why is this road in poor condition?"
- "How much budget was allocated here?"
- "Who is the contractor?"
- "File a complaint for this issue"

Judge angle:

- natural-language access to governance data
- citizen-friendly explanation layer

## 4) Transparency module (1 min)

Open full transparency module and show mismatch alerts:

- "INR 5,000,000 allocated but score is 38/100"

Judge angle:

- spend vs outcome accountability
- contractor and repair history traceability

## 5) Complaint timeline + offline (1 min)

1. Open Complaints tab and show status progression.
2. Mention offline queue mode (capture + complaint local storage).
3. Tap sync action to show deferred submission.

Judge angle:

- usable on low-connectivity roads
- practical for real citizens

## 6) Intelligence + prediction (1 min)

1. Open Intelligence tab.
2. Show trend chart and risk prediction output.
3. Explain "may deteriorate in X days" signal for preventive maintenance.

Judge angle:

- predictive governance
- data-backed maintenance planning
